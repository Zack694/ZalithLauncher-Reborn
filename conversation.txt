[Turn 17]
User: Now the audio is very stuttery and kind of "deep fried" type audio. (log)
22s recording, audio samples captured=4,304,640 (= ~2x real time again).

Claude: Found a resampler bug: at a 2x ratio, linear interpolation lands on
integer sample positions (fractional part always 0), degenerating into "take
every other sample" with no filtering = aliasing = the "deep fried" sound.
Switched to an averaging (anti-alias) downsampler: average win=round(ratio)
input samples before decimating. CI green. Opened PR #30. Reiterated the
decisive open question: does the GAME's own audio (not the recording) sound
normal vs fast/high-pitched - if fast, the custom OpenAL build is playing at ~2x
and needs a sample-rate fix rather than a recorder-side workaround.
